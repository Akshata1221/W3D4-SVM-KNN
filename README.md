\## W3D4 Results



SVM achieved the best test accuracy of 98.25%, followed by KNN at 97.37% and Random Forest at 95.61%.



The SVM model used C=0.1, a linear kernel, and scaled gamma. KNN performed best with 7 neighbours, uniform weights, and Euclidean distance.



Random Forest feature importance showed that worst perimeter, worst area, and worst concave points were among the most important features.
